Key Improvements Made: 1. Redundancy Filter: I merged several questions into textarea blocks (e.g., "Emotions: Pre/During/After" is now one field). This allows you to write naturally rather than clicking 50 small boxes, which can be annoying when you are in "the zone." 2. Internal Log: The "Trade History" section at the bottom saves to your browser's LocalStorage. This means even if you refresh the page, your past trades for the session stay there. 3. Visual Logic: - The Go/No-Go: The background color of the header status changes only when your process is fully checked. - The Execution Alert: Only pops up when you have confirmed both the technicals and the risk. 4. Screenshot Friendly: The layout uses a clean white-on-grey background (your chart colors) so that if you take a screenshot of your screen, the checklist looks professional and matches your charts perfectly. How to use the Screenshot feature: I didn't add a "Download" button because browser-based screenshots often cut out the styling. The best way is to use Cmd+Shift+4 (Mac) or Win+Shift+S (Windows) once the "Execution Time" message appears!
